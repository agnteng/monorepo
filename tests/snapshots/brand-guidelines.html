06 — Voice & usage
The voice

Plainspoken, specific, builder-to-builder, mildly wry. Concrete over grandiose. We earn trust by respecting intelligence, not by shouting.

say “Notes from the first Agentic Engineering meetup.”
say “Six talks, one room, what actually shipped.”
not “Unlock the future of agentic AI. 🚀”
Motion is required

Almost no meetup bothers to move or illustrate — which is exactly the opening. If nothing moves and nothing is drawn, it reads as a template. Every surface gets the live dither or an illustrated header. Never static, never stock.
